01 — PROJECT OVERVIEW

CENTRALIZED CRISIS DISPATCH

The Crisis Response Management platform presents a web-based approach intended to help individuals and organizations participate effectively in disaster relief efforts.

01

IDENTIFY

Recognize critical emergency scenarios and access structured disaster information.

02

REPORT

Submit location coordinates and situation descriptions to log active crisis incidents.

03

CONNECT & RESPOND

Register volunteer availability and access dedicated pathways for emergency services.

02 — THE PROBLEM

CHALLENGES IN EMERGENCY COORDINATION

01

DISASTER REPORTING

People in crisis need a quick, accessible way to report a disaster and provide situation details.

02

VOLUNTEER COORDINATION

Willing volunteers lack a structured platform to register their availability and skill sets.

03

EMERGENCY ACCESS

During crisis events, users require rapid access to emergency contact categories like ambulance and police.

04

INFORMATION ACCESS

Disaster-response resources are often scattered across fragmented, non-centralized channels.

07 — TECHNICAL HONESTY

DESIGN EVOLUTION & CURRENT LIMITATIONS

PROTOTYPE CONCEPT

FROM CLASS PROJECT TO CONCEPT

The original project was developed as a web prototype/concept to bring disaster reporting, volunteering, and emergency guidance into one unified interface.

CURRENT LIMITATIONS

TECHNICAL TRANSPARENCY

  • No verified real-time disaster data feed
  • No automated emergency-service integration
  • No automated volunteer matching algorithms
  • No production database or live backend
  • No automated emergency push notifications
