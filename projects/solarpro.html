01 — PROJECT OVERVIEW

SMART SOLAR TRACKING & TELEMETRY

Solar energy is one of the cleanest and most abundant renewable resources. However, fixed solar panels are not always aligned with the sun, which can reduce energy generation efficiency. SolarPro addresses this by combining physical dual-axis solar tracking with real-time web telemetry.

01

AUTOMATED TRACKING

Continuously senses sunlight orientation and rotates the solar plate toward the brightest position.

02

ENERGY MEASUREMENT

Measures and records voltage and power output generated by the solar panel array.

03

WEB MONITORING

Transmits telemetry data to a live web interface for real-time visualization and analytics.

02 — THE PROBLEM

LIMITATIONS OF FIXED SOLAR INSTALLATIONS

01

FIXED PANEL ALIGNMENT

Fixed solar panels cannot continuously adjust their orientation according to the changing position of the sun throughout the day.

02

REDUCED EFFICIENCY

Improper alignment with the sun reduces the total intensity of sunlight received, leading to lower energy generation efficiency.

03

LIMITED MONITORING

Users lack convenient, transparent methods to monitor real-time power generation and energy usage.

FIXED PANEL LIMITATION FIXED ORIENTATION ➔ VARIABLE SUN POSITION ➔ MISALIGNED PANEL ➔ REDUCED EFFICIENCY

05 — HOW IT WORKS

OPERATIONAL WORKFLOW

01

DETECT

LDR sensors detect the direction of maximum sunlight intensity.

02

PROCESS

Arduino microcontrollers calculate analog sensor differential values.

03

ROTATE

The motor rotates the solar plate smoothly toward the brightest position.

04

MEASURE

Power generation and energy metrics are measured and recorded.

05

MONITOR

Collected telemetry data is transmitted to the web interface for live monitoring.
